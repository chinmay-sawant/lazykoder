What we do

  1. Preflight sees used > 800,000 and starts compact.
  2. Walk backward. Keep about 15k tokens of recent messages (plus the last user turn, whole messages only). That is the tail.
  3. The other ~785k is the head. Send it to the summarizer with compact.md. If one request cannot hold 785k, we chunk, summarize each chunk, then summarize those summaries into one checkpoint.
  4. New summary is still one handoff, typically 1–4k tokens, hard cap 4,096. It does not stay 785k or 800k.
  5. Next send ≈ 4k summary + 15k tail ≈ 16–20k.

Why the tail overshoots 15k

We add whole messages from the end until the pile is at least 15,000. If we are at 14,000 and the next older message is 6,000, we keep it and the tail becomes 20,000. We never slice a message in half. That is the whole reason your tail was ~19.6k instead of 15,000, and why 1.3k + 19.6k reads as ~21k.
